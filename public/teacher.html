Quick teacher guide

  1. Create a room. Press "Create a room". A code, link and QR code appear.
  2. Show the join screen. Press "TV view" for a big code and QR code.
  3. Pupils join. They scan the QR code, open the link, or type the code.
  4. Press Start. Each pupil answers two English questions, reads the feedback, then plays a merge round. Everyone gets a round, right or wrong.
  5. Watch the Pupils tab. English results and game results are separate.
  6. Before you finish, export CSV. Nothing is stored permanently.

Helping a pupil who is stuck

  • In the Pupils tab, switch on Simple merge. Matching items get highlighted.
  • Switch on English only to skip the merge rounds entirely.
  • Pupils have a Hint button, a Recycle button and a Helping hand button that appears if they ever run out of moves.

What survives what

  • Phone refresh: everything. The pupil returns to the same board.
  • Short Wi-Fi drop: everything. The page reconnects by itself.
  • Server restart or redeploy: nothing. Rooms and results are lost. Export CSV first.
  • A later lesson: nothing carries over. Make a new room.
